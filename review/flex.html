01 / THE DEAD SPACE

190 in a 2560 window. The card is not the problem.

The 150 focus table is a 1000×1000 window with a 900px board. On a 2560×1440 screen it uses 39% of the width and 69% of the height. The board caps at 900, the card caps at 190, and the rest of the screen is margin. At 1440×900 the 1000 square cannot fit at all; the current 960 fallback still needs 960.

1000 × 1000 focus window 900px board · 190px card 1,660px unused at 2560

THE HARD CAP

900px

The board has a fixed maximum width. A wider window does not widen the mats.

THE FIXED WINDOW

1000 square

The table forces a square window instead of filling the screen it was given.

THE COST

35% width

At 2560px, 1,660px is untouched. At 720px high, the 1000 window overflows by 280px.

02 / THE BUDGET

One card. Two vertical budgets.

The card is 5:7. The only question is how many card heights the window can carry. The bottom hand costs three card heights: two mats plus the hand. The side hand costs two: the hand moves into a two-column fan on the right.

BOTTOM HAND

3 card heights

card_h = (H − 202) / 3. Simple, readable hand; the wide sides stay empty.

SIDE HAND

2 card heights

card_h = (H − 198) / 2 plus a 2×3 hand fan. The width budget also constrains it.

CHROME

Fixed 150%

Score 82, dock 64, targets 48, tab 52. The card size is the only thing that flexes.

At 2560×1440 the bottom budget gives 294px; the side budget gives 443px. At 1000×1000 the side hand loses to width: 152px versus 190px for the bottom hand. That is why the recommendation changes with the window shape, not a single breakpoint.

06 / PICK

One adaptive table. Two hand positions.

The table should not force a square or move the window. It should fill the window it has. The best card budget is the side hand in landscape and the bottom hand in square.

RECOMMENDATION

Hybrid: C in landscape.
B in square.

Keep the 150% chrome. Compute one 5:7 card size from the window. Use the 2-column side hand when width ÷ height ≥ 1.5; use the bottom hand when the window is square or portrait. The card is 186 at 1280×720, 240 at 1440×900, 315 at 1920×1080, and 443 at 2560×1440. At 1000×1000 the bottom hand returns 190 / 180.

  • Fixed A: baseline only; 190 at 1000×1000; 35% width at 2560.
  • Fluid B: bottom hand; 123–294; the safe fallback.
  • Fluid C: 2-column side hand; 144–443; max fill in landscape.
  • Docked D: bottom hand + rail; 123–294; logs always visible.
  • Never: hide a lane, shrink a card inside a table, or force a 1000 square on a 900-high screen.
  • Drawer: left overlay in C; right overlay in B; docked right in D.
PORT STEP 1

Fill the window

Stop setting a fixed 1000/960 size. The table root uses the current window; the stage is the window minus no chrome.

PORT STEP 2

Solve the token

Compute the card from the height and width budget. One token per table; all zones share it. Chrome stays at 150%.

PORT STEP 3

Switch the hand

Landscape: 2-column side hand, drawer left. Square: bottom hand, drawer right. 6th card scrolls or fans.

This review chose the fluid full-window direction. The live X2 table now fills the window at 150% chrome, keeps all three lanes, and scales the same card token. The old fixed 190 / 1000 focus is historical.
