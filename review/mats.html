01 / WHAT WAS REMOVED

The labels were the clutter.

The table repeated its own state in words. The mat pass removes the subtitle and keeps the signal. Every state below is now read by shape, count, edge or residue.

I · II · III→the three columns; position is the lane
OPEN→an empty ghost card with a plus
CALL→the plus is the call target
GUARDED→the enemy card; a lock pip on the mat
◆ 1 GUARD · FACE SEALED→three lock pips; fill = guard count
NEXT · STRIKE · LANE I→a pink target bracket on the threatened card
DAWN · DRAW · CALL · ACT · CLASH · DUSK→six ticks on the center seam; one is lit
HAND 5 · DECK 34→five cards; one stack with a number
ALL LANES

No tabs, no focus lane, no hidden row. The three columns are the game. They stay on the table at every size.

TWO MATS

Opponent mat and player mat have different materials. The seam is the table; the hand and actions live on the table edge.

THE HIT

The card is the target. A 64–110px number lands on it; the mat edge and score respond; the scar stays. No log sentence.
