02 / THE TELL

Anticipation you can read.

The back is not a slot machine. It is a label. The pips, ring, and word are read from the card that is already inside the folio.

This rack shows the whole grammar at once. In the folio, hover or focus a back to read it. Legendary adds a double ring and a four-pip diamond. No colour-only signals; no fake near-miss.

03 / THE TEN BEATS

Short, physical, repeatable.

Every beat has a beginning, an impact, and a residue. The whole 5-card ritual lands in roughly four seconds when you flip quickly; reading is player-controlled.

04 / THE LEDGER

The reward is the receipt.

The summary keeps the fan on screen. It counts what you got, fills the set gauge, and tells you what to do next without hiding the cards.
